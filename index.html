I'm a Backend Engineer from Mansoura, Egypt, finishing my B.Sc. in Computer & Information Sciences. I build systems the way I'd want to maintain them a year from now — layered cleanly, so business rules never depend on a framework or a database to make sense.

★ Featured Project
TRADESPHERE
ERP for Import & Distribution

TradeSphere is an ERP backend for an import and distribution company — it tracks goods from the moment a purchase is agreed with a supplier through shipping, customs clearance, quality inspection, warehousing, sale, delivery, and financial settlement on both ends.

Built on Clean Architecture with a strict inward dependency direction, CQRS through MediatR, and domain-driven building blocks — rich entities that enforce their own status transitions and raise domain events instead of leaning on scattered service logic.

The interesting part is what happens automatically: a shipment marked as arrived opens a customs clearance case on its own, a completed quality inspection updates inventory and closes the purchase order, and confirming an order or delivering a sale opens the right payment record — all through domain events, without one module ever calling into another directly.
